Automated Plant Machinery

FROM MATERIAL TO BUILT STRENGTH

Industrial automated hydraulic cycle at our Muthalipalayam, Arasur facility guarantees pristine rectangularity and high compressive compaction.

Brixion Industrial Process Flow
precision_manufacturing AUTOMATED PRODUCTION CYCLE SCHEMATIC
ZERO KILN FLUE EMISSIONS
Continuous Automated Cycling Muthalipalayam, Arasur Facility
STAGE 01 RAW INVENTORY

Class-F Fly Ash & Binder Batching

Pulverized fuel ash, 53-grade Ordinary Portland Cement, graded river aggregate, and water are mixed under automated load-cell sensors for uniform moisture.

STAGE 02 MULTI-TONNAGE COMPACTION

Hydraulic High-Pressure Moulding

Automated hydraulic block press compresses the aggregate mix with dual-vibration cycles, squeezing out microscopic air voids and honeycombing.

STAGE 03 HYDRATION BONDING

Precision Non-Combustion Curing

Bricks are cured through calibrated water showering and steam stabilization for 14-21 days, gaining permanent stone-like compressive molecular bonds without kiln fuel.

STAGE 04 LOGISTICS QA

Palletized Dispatch & Batch Testing

Random batch units undergo compressive crushing tests in our plant laboratory before heavy flatbed loading for jobsite courier across Coimbatore.
